  4. The rule is not hidden at cell 0; it is locally readable at every position in the grid from $(in\_chars[i] + i) \pmod 2$. A radius-0 model (no spatial communication) achieves 100% accuracy.
  5. Furthermore, cells $i > 8$ are outside the 8-tick causal light cone of cell 0, yet are evaluated with mask 1.0. They succeed solely because of this local parity leak.
- **Severity**: **Fatal**

## Actionable Directives for Engineering & Research Agents

1. **Fix `Ambiguous Basin`**: Expand beyond 2 fixed sequences (`"A:X="` vs `"B:X="`). Generate random variable-length context strings and varied ambiguous cues so the task tests attractor selection rather than 1-bit memorization.
2. **Fix `Bracket Depth`**: Eliminate the negative drift and parity-locked branching in `src/tasks.rs:260-275`. Balance opening and closing probabilities, avoid forcing decrements on final steps, and ensure final depth varies uniformly across $\{0, 1, 2, \dots, \text{max\_depth}\}$.
3. **Randomize `Parity Tracking`**: Generate independent Bernoulli bits $b_i \sim \text{Bernoulli}(0.5)$ via seeded PRNG. Eliminate the alternating $(sample\_idx + i) \pmod 2$ formula so cumulative parity is balanced $P(\text{odd}) = 0.5$.
4. **Generalize `Sequence Reversal`**: Sample random strings from full ASCII/alphanumeric vocabulary rather than sliding an 8-character alphabet window.
5. **Decouple `Hidden Rule`**: Generate digits independently from $is\_add$. Ensure $P(digit \mid is\_add) = 0.1$ uniformly so cells cannot infer the rule locally from $(digit + i) \pmod 2$.
6. **Randomize `Associative Completion`**: Randomly bind keys to values per sample so the model cannot memorize a static lookup table (`k=1, m=2, p=3, r=4`).
7. **Expand `Delayed Recall` Universe**: Eliminate the $\gcd(3, 6) = 3$ collapse to 2 sequences. Sample memory sequences randomly from a unified vocabulary shared between training and validation.
8. **Wire Loss Masking into `cmd_benchmark`**: Use `masked_cross_entropy_loss` and `masked_accuracy` in `cmd_benchmark` when evaluating synthetic task batches.
9. **Complete Invalidation Segregation**: Move (do not copy) all 9 legacy checkpoints out of root `checkpoints/` and into `invalidated_results/checkpoints/`. Update `PROVENANCE.md` to include `m1_sanity` and all defective synthetic tasks.
10. **Implement Rigorous Baseline Training & Seeding**: Pass seeds to `train_baseline_model`, seed `VarMap` weights, and train for sufficient gradient steps (e.g. 500+ steps) before reporting architectural comparisons.
11. **Implement Causal Left-Only Stencils**: Replace central spatial stencils in `src/nca.rs` with causal backward differences ($\nabla_L x_i = x_i - x_{i-1}$, $\Delta_L x_i = x_i - 2x_{i-1} + x_{i-2}$).
